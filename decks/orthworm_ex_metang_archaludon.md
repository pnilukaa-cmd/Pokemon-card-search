# Orthworm ex / Metang: Archaludon ex and Genesect ex

Reviewed 2026-10-29 from a submitted list ("Chef's version"): 3 Orthworm ex
on a 4-3-1 Beldum / Metang TEF / Metagross CRI line, with Metang's Metal
Maker loading 16 Basic Metal Energy, Fezandipiti ex, Heavy Baton, Hero's
Cape, Jumbo Ice Cream, Full Metal Lab, Energy Recycler, Team Rocket's
Petrel and Brock's Scouting. Asked with it: which strong Metal Pokémon to
add to a mix like this?

**This list: 78.00% against the 101-deck field at 1000 games per opponent**
(median 80.2%, 94 of 101 winning), on the engine as of b6759de. On the
same seeds it is **+19.72 ± 1.00** over the submitted list (58.28%), better
on 99 of 101.

**The changes:**
- **+2 Archaludon ex and 2 Duraludon** (Stage 1 ex, 300 HP).
- **+2 Genesect ex** for the 2 Team Rocket's Petrel.
- **−Metagross**, −2 Heavy Baton, −2 Energy Recycler, −2 Jumbo Ice Cream
  CRI 109; +3 Basic Metal Energy (19).

The submitted Boss's Orders LOR-TG 24 is a rotated printing (Lost Origin
is not in Standard); this pool carries MEG 114, and the import uses it.
Fezandipiti ex ASC 288, Energy Recycler DRI 164, Night Stretcher ASC 196,
Lillie's Determination ASC 192, Brock's Scouting JTG 179 and Buddy-Buddy
Poffin ASC 184 are real printings the pool doesn't carry; the import keeps
yours.

## The strategy, card by card

- **Metang** (TEF 114, Stage 1, 100 HP): **Metal Maker**, once a turn: look
  at the top 4 and attach every Basic Metal Energy there, to any of your
  Pokémon. The other Metang printings (CRI 60, JTG 62) don't have it.
- **Orthworm ex** (SCR 110, Basic, 220 HP, Retreat 4): **Pummeling
  Payback** puts 2 damage counters on the attacker for each Metal Energy
  attached, whenever it is damaged, even by the Knock Out. **Rock Tomb**
  (4 Colorless): 150, and the Defending Pokémon can't retreat.
- **Archaludon ex** (SSP 130, Stage 1 from Duraludon, 300 HP):
  **Assemble Alloy** attaches up to 2 Basic Metal Energy from the discard
  when it evolves. **Metal Defender** (3 Metal): 220, and no Weakness
  during the opponent's next turn.
- **Genesect ex** (BLK 161, Basic, 220 HP): **Metallic Signal** searches up
  to 2 Evolution Metal Pokémon a turn: a Metang and an Archaludon ex.
  **Protect Charge** (Metal, Metal, Colorless): 150, −30 next turn.
- **Full Metal Lab:** Metal Pokémon take 30 less from attacks (both
  players'). **Jumbo Ice Cream:** heal 80 from an Active with 3 or more
  Energy. **Hero's Cape:** +100 HP.

**What it adds up to:**
- **The strong Metal Pokémon to add are Archaludon ex, Genesect ex and
  Mega Mawile ex**, in that order. Mega Excadrill ex is close behind on its
  own, but adds little once the first two are in (+0.25).
  - **Archaludon ex** is a second attacker that doesn't spend Metal Maker's
    Energy: Assemble Alloy brings 2 back from the discard as it evolves,
    and Metal Defender does 220 from 300 HP.
  - **Genesect ex** is search. Metallic Signal finds a Metang and an
    Archaludon ex every turn, which Team Rocket's Petrel (a Trainer) and
    Brock's Scouting (Basics) can't.
  - Mega Skarmory ex, Dialga ex and Zacian ex were small; Hop's Zacian ex,
    Cobalion ex, Scizor ex and Heatran lost.
- **Metagross costs the deck its engine.** Metagross has no Metal Maker,
  so every Metang evolved into it is an Energy engine off the board. The
  deck wants Metang to stay Metang. One Metagross for Metal Energy: +5.27,
  and +5.70 with Genesect ex in.
- **Heavy Baton and Energy Recycler did least.** Heavy Baton needs an
  Active Orthworm ex Knocked Out with Retreat exactly 4. Energy Recycler
  measured even with a Metal Energy.
- **Magnetic Metal Energy** (no Retreat Cost for its Metal holder) does fix
  Orthworm ex's Retreat 4, but measured −0.33 for 2: Metal Maker only
  attaches Basic Metal Energy, and it turns Heavy Baton off.
- **This simulator's AI runs short of Energy** (see
  `jellicent_ex_oceanic_curse.md`, *Boss's Orders, re-measured*), so the
  "Metal Energy for X" rows lean toward the Energy. The Jumbo Ice Cream,
  one-Orthworm and Brock's Scouting cuts rest on that kind of row; only the
  Jumbo one, confirmed at 1000 games, is in this list. The Archaludon ex,
  Genesect ex and Metagross results don't depend on it.

## How to play it

1. **Turn 1.**
   - Beldum, Orthworm ex or Genesect ex Active; Buddy-Buddy Poffin for two
     Beldum (70 HP).
   - Genesect ex on the Bench: Metallic Signal for a Metang and an
     Archaludon ex. No evolving on either player's first turn.
2. **Turn 2.**
   - Evolve Beldum into Metang and Duraludon into Archaludon ex (Assemble
     Alloy needs Basic Metal Energy in the discard: Ultra Ball is the way).
   - Metal Maker on each Metang. Load the Active Orthworm ex: every Metal
     Energy is 2 counters back on whatever hits it.
3. **Turn 3 and after.**
   - Rock Tomb (150, no retreat for them) from Orthworm ex, or Metal
     Defender (220) from Archaludon ex.
   - Keep Metang as Metang. Full Metal Lab for −30.

**The opponent's outs:**
- **Fire.** Every Metal Pokémon in the list is weak to it.
- **Ability lock** stops Metal Maker and Metallic Signal together.
- **Gusting a Metang** (100 HP) takes the engine.
- **Full Metal Lab helps their Metal Pokémon too.**

## What the simulator found

1. **Brock's Scouting's other half was never read**: "up to 2 Basic
   Pokémon or 1 Evolution Pokémon" compiled as 2 Basics only. Taking the
   Metang once three Pokémon were down measured −0.68 here and −0.28 /
   −0.34 on the field's two other Metal lists, so the simulator still takes
   the Basics; noted at the rule.
2. **The setup baseline ran only draw Abilities** and held Brock's Scouting
   and Energy Recycler in hand (b6759de). Metal Maker, the Scouting and the
   Recycler took the submitted list's first attack by turn 6 from 72.7% of
   trials to 93.7%. Genesect ex's Metallic Signal (the commit after) took
   this list's Metang in play by turn 6 from 70.3% to 86.7%. Each has a
   test that fails on the commit before.

**Anti-synergies:**
- Metagross evolves away a Metal Maker.
- Magnetic Metal Energy turns Heavy Baton off (Retreat must be exactly 4),
  and Metal Maker can't attach it.
- Full Metal Lab shields the opponent's Metal Pokémon too.
