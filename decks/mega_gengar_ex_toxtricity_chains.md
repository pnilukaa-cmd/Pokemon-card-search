# Mega Gengar ex / Gengar ex / Toxtricity Chains

Reviewed 2026-10-16 from a submitted list: Gastly → Haunter → Gengar ex and
Mega Gengar ex, with Team Rocket's Nidorina's Dark Awakening to evolve,
Toxtricity for Energy, Pecharunt ex's Subjugating Chains to switch, and
Munkidori to move damage counters. The submitted Energy line was cut off at
"10 Da"; it was read as 10 Basic Darkness Energy, which makes 60 cards.

**This list: 76.35% against the 93-deck field at 1000 games per opponent**
(median 81.1%, 85 of 93 winning). On the same seeds:
- **+23.53 ± 0.75** over the submitted list (52.82%), better on all 93.
- **+10.72 ± 0.43** over the round-3 list with the Nidoran / Nidorina
  engine put back (65.64%), better on all 93.

The submitted list measured 37.22% once fixes 1-2 below were in. Fixes 3-7
took it to 52.82%; the list changes took it the rest of the way.

**Lines:** Gastly → Haunter → Gengar ex / Mega Gengar ex (Rare Candy too),
Toxel → Toxtricity, and Pecharunt ex. Every Pokémon is Darkness and every
attack is paid with Darkness Energy.

## What changed from the submitted list, and why

| Change | Measured (200 games a matchup, paired) |
|---|---|
| −2-2 Team Rocket's Nidoran♀ / Nidorina, +4 Energy | +26.46 ± 0.74, better on all 93. Putting them back into the round-3 list: −9.38 ± 0.41 at 1000 games |
| −2 Munkidori, −2 Drayton, +4 Energy | +3.00 ± 0.53 |
| −1 Energy, +1 Dawn (3), Hero's Cape for Unfair Stamp | +1.34 ± 0.21 at 1000 games |

The list kept everything else. Round after round, the same thing came out:
**the deck runs out of Energy.** Both attackers cost two Darkness, Void Gale
moves one of the attacker's Energy off every time it's used. Even at 14
Energy (the list without the Nidorina line), most logged turns without an
attack had no Energy in hand. 17-18 measured best: −2 more Poké Pad for 2 Energy was
−0.99, and every Trainer added for an Energy lost too, except the third
Dawn.

**Why the Nidorina line goes.** Dark Awakening (D) evolves up to 2 of your
Darkness Pokémon from the deck, but Nidorina has to be in the Active Spot
to use it, it does no damage, and Rare Candy already takes Gastly straight
to Gengar ex. The line costs 4 slots, a 70 HP Basic you sometimes start
with, and a 90 HP Active that attacks for 0. It was used about 0.4 times a
game. Letting the AI choose what Dark Awakening evolves (a Haunter into
Gengar ex before a Nidoran into Nidorina) measured −0.25 ± 0.48, so the
choice wasn't the problem.

**Why Munkidori goes.** Adrena-Brain (move up to 3 damage counters from
your Pokémon to theirs) needs Darkness Energy on Munkidori, and Munkidori
is a Psychic Pokémon:
- Sinister Surge can't reach it (Benched Darkness Pokémon only), so its
  Energy costs your one manual attachment of the turn.
- Its attack, Mind Bend, needs Psychic Energy and can't be paid at all.
- Its fuel is Sinister Surge's 2 counters, 3 at most a turn.

Cutting both for Energy measured +2.48 ± 0.45. Putting one back into the
final shell measured −0.71.

## How to play

1. **Turn 1.** Buddy-Buddy Poffin for Gastly and Toxel (both 70 HP), and
   Bench a Pecharunt ex. Attach a Darkness Energy.
2. **Turn 2.**
   - Rare Candy a Gastly into **Mega Gengar ex** or **Gengar ex** (not on
     your first turn, and not on a Gastly played this turn).
   - Evolve Toxel into **Toxtricity**. **Sinister Surge** attaches a Basic
     Darkness Energy from the deck to a Benched Darkness Pokémon and puts 2
     damage counters on it. Put it on the attacker you'll use next.
   - **Subjugating Chains** (Pecharunt ex, once a turn) switches that
     Benched attacker into the Active Spot. It's now Poisoned, but the one
     it replaced goes to the Bench, where Poison ends.
3. **Every turn after.**
   - **Mega Gengar ex's Void Gale** (DD): 230, and move an Energy from it
     to one of your Benched Pokémon. Send it to the Gengar ex or Mega that
     attacks next; Sinister Surge adds the second.
   - **Gengar ex's Chaotic Pain** (DD): put 13 damage counters on any of
     their Pokémon. Use it to take a Benched Pokémon ex they're building,
     or to finish one that's damaged.
   - Chains swaps the two attackers every turn. A Mega Gengar ex that just
     used Void Gale has one Energy left; it gets its second back on the
     Bench.
   - **Pecharunt ex's Irritated Outburst** (DD) is 60 for each Prize your
     opponent has taken: 240-300 late in the game.
   - **Hero's Cape** is +100 HP. A Mega Gengar ex at 450 is out of reach of
     most single attacks; a Toxtricity at 240 is harder to drag up and
     Knock Out. In the simulator it went mostly onto Toxtricity and
     Pecharunt ex.

**Mega Gengar ex's Shadowy Concealment** (Ability): if one of your Darkness
Pokémon is Knocked Out by an attack from your opponent's **Pokémon ex**,
they take 1 fewer Prize. Every Pokémon here is Darkness, so against an ex
deck:
- Mega Gengar ex gives up 2 (not 3), Gengar ex and Pecharunt ex give up 1,
  and Gastly, Haunter, Toxel and Toxtricity give up nothing.
- It doesn't stack with a second Mega Gengar ex.
- It does nothing against a Pokémon without a Rule Box.

**The opponent's outs:**
- **Fighting.** Every Pokémon in the list is weak to Fighting.
- **Single-Prize attackers** ignore Shadowy Concealment.
- **Knock Out Mega Gengar ex first.** With it gone, Gengar ex is 2 Prizes
  again.
- **Ability lock** stops Sinister Surge and Subjugating Chains, and the
  deck falls back to one manual attachment a turn for two-Energy attacks.
- **Gust the Toxtricity** or Pecharunt ex while the attackers wait.

**Matchups (1000 games):**
- **Worst:** Mega Zygarde ex / Cinderace 21.4% (Fighting) and 47.1%, Mega
  Zeraora ex / Cinderace 33.4%, Tauros / Risky Ruins 34.3%, Arboliva /
  Ogerpon 34.5%, Mega Absol ex hand lock 44.3%, Munkidori ex / Pecharunt ex
  49.1%.
- **Best:** Mew baby box 99.3%, Team Rocket's Wobbuffet 97.4%, Mew / Hypno
  97.1%, Salazzle ex 95.1%.
- Against the other recent decks: Alakazam / Munkidori 68.8%, Aggron /
  Diggersby 68.7%, Gourgeist ex / Diggersby 65.2%.

## What the simulator found

Seven engine problems, all fixed with regression tests that fail on the
commit before. The first two were in this list's own cards:
1. **Chaotic Pain** put all 13 counters on the Active even with a 2-Prize
   Knock Out sitting on the Bench, and the AI priced it at a flat 130.
2. **Void Gale's Energy move** didn't happen: Mega Gengar ex kept both
   Energy and swung for 230 every turn.
3. **Shadowy Concealment applied to its owner's own Knock Outs too.** Mega
   Gengar ex Knocking Out a Darkness Pokémon ex took 1 Prize, not 2. A
   "take 1 more Prize" card had the mirror problem.
4. **Attacks whose damage is all counters ranked as 0 damage** when the AI
   handed out Energy. Gengar ex lost Energy to Toxtricity. The same went
   for "220 damage to 1 of your opponent's Pokémon" with no printed number
   (Mega Skarmory ex, Kyurem). +4.71 ± 0.51 on the submitted list.
5. **Sinister Surge spread its Energy.** It went to the emptiest Benched
   Pokémon, so one a turn landed on Gengar ex, Pecharunt ex, Toxtricity
   and a second Gengar ex, and none of them ever reached two. Chains had
   nothing to switch in and the Active Mega Gengar ex sat at 0 Energy for
   five turns. It now goes to the hardest hitter still short. **+10.46 ±
   0.53** on the submitted list, better on 89 of 93.
6. **Four "look at the top N cards and attach the Energy you find there"
   cards attached nothing** (Metang's Metal Maker, Grumpig, Lapras ex,
   Waitress). They put one card into the hand instead.
7. **"Attach up to 2 Energy to 1 of your Stage 2 Pokémon"** (Rosa's
   Encouragement) could load any Pokémon and split the two. Philippe and
   Mewtwo split too.

Fixes 4-7 change field decks too: the Metang / Excadrill decks, the
Dragapult decks (Rosa's Encouragement), Orthworm (Philippe), the Raging
Bolt and Tauros decks (Glass Trumpet) and the Hydreigon mill deck. Their own
notes still carry their older numbers until the next full field run.

**Screened against the final list** (200 games, paired), all below a Basic
Darkness Energy in the same slot:
- Energy Search for 2 Poké Pad −0.29, 2 Waitress for 2 Energy −0.89 (for
  a Lillie's and a Poké Pad −2.52), Energy Retrieval −1.14, 2 Rosa's
  Encouragement −1.38, Powerglass −1.66.
- Earlier rounds: Janine's Secret Art, Legacy Energy, Shadowy Darkness
  Energy, Risky Ruins, Punk Helmet, Gengar ex TEF 104, Haunter POR 49 (see
  the table).

**Anti-synergies:**
- Subjugating Chains Poisons your own new Active. Switching or evolving it
  ends the Poison; leaving it Active for a turn costs 10.
- Sinister Surge's 2 counters land on your own Pokémon, every turn it's
  used (5 times a game in the logs). They go on the attacker it loads, so
  a 350 HP Mega Gengar ex takes them easily; a 70 HP Gastly doesn't.
- Shadowy Concealment doesn't cover the Psychic Munkidori, one more reason
  it went.
- Gengar ex's Fainting Spell (a coin flip to Knock Out the attacker when
  it's Knocked Out) is a bonus, not a plan.
