# Sleep: every card in the pool (2026-10-05)

**Rule check (engine agrees):** an Asleep Pokemon can't attack or retreat,
and flips at every Pokemon Checkup -- heads, it wakes. Sleep you inflict
on your turn gets a flip at the end of THAT turn, so it stops their next
attack only half the time. Switching, evolving, and Festival Grounds /
Bubbly Water / Ancient Booster cure or block it. Sleep is unreliable as a
lock and strong as a **condition to cash in the same turn**.

## Producers
- **Shiinotic SSP 194** (Stage 1, 110 HP), Calming Light: an Ability, so free and once a turn, with no attack used. It only works while Shiinotic is Active.
- **Attacks that put the opponent to sleep:**
  - Exeggcute 30C 1 (Hypnosis, C).
  - Foongus JTG 10 (Spore Ball, G, 10).
  - Parasect JTG 5 (Spore, C).
  - Snom TEF 168 and Spheal SSP 199 (Powder Snow, W, 10).
  - Poliwhirl and Poliwrath TWM (Hypnosis, W).
  - Team Rocket's Drowzee (Hypnotic Ray, P, 10).
  - Amaura POR 23 (Icy Wind, WC, 50).
  - Musharna BLK 117 (Sleep Pulse, P, 50).
  - Amoonguss ex JTG 11 (Spore Ball, G, 30).
  - Frosmoth MEG 43 (Chilling Wings, W: 20 to each of their Pokémon).
  - Dustox ASC 15 (Twilight Poison, GG, 100: Asleep and Poisoned).
  - Vileplume PFL 3 (Pollen Bomb, G, 30: Asleep and Poisoned).
  - Erika's Vileplume ex ASC 3 (Bloom Powder, GGC, 160: Asleep and Poisoned).
  - Milotic ex SSP 217 (Hypno Splash, WCC, 160).
  - Mega Froslass ex ASC 265 (Absolute Snow, WCC, 150).
- **Komala JTG 129** (Slumbering Smack, C, 30): both Active Pokémon fall asleep, and Komala hits for +100 next turn.
- **Team Rocket's Hypnotizer** (Tool): when its Team Rocket's holder is damaged while Active, the attacker falls asleep.

## Payoffs (any Special Condition counts)
- **Mega Darkrai ex PBL 101** (Basic, Mega, 280 HP), Abyss Eye (DDD): Knocks Out an Active that is affected by any Special Condition.
- **Amoonguss BLK 11**, Dangerous Reaction (C): 30, or 150 against an affected Active.
- **Walking Wake ex TEF 189**, Catharsis Roar (WCC): 120, or 240 against an affected Active.
- **Cradily SCR 6** and **Team Rocket's Muk DRI 124**: 100 damage for each Special Condition on their Active.
- **Mismagius ASC 86**, Assassin's Magic: if their Active is affected, 6 damage counters on one of their Benched Pokémon.

## The best line
**Shiinotic + Mega Darkrai ex: Calming Light, then Switch into Darkrai, then Abyss Eye.** That Knocks Out any Active Pokémon, ex or Mega included, every turn the loop runs. Its costs:
- A switching card every turn (Switch, Prime Catcher), or Air Balloon on Shiinotic.
- Three Darkness Energy on Darkrai. Janine's Secret Art attaches 2 from the deck.
- Darkrai gives up 3 Prizes when Knocked Out (Mega rule).
- Shiinotic has to survive in the Active Spot.

A simpler, colourless version: **Komala, then Amoonguss BLK**, or **Foongus/Exeggcute into Amoonguss** (150 for one Energy).

## Engine fixes from this study
- **Self-inflicted Sleep hit the opponent instead.** "This Pokémon is now Asleep" compiled onto the opponent's Active, so Wailord ex, Snorlax, Munna, Pikachu and Larry's Komala each got a free Sleep lock, and Pangoro's Confusion drawback flipped the same way.
- **Komala's Slumbering Smack slept nobody.**

## Pilot limit (not fixed)
- **The greedy pilot can't play the Shiinotic loop.**
  - Over 20 logged games of a draft list, Calming Light fired 38 times and Abyss Eye 0.
  - A late-Switch rule never triggered, because Switch was already spent and Darkrai was rarely ready on the Bench. That rule was reverted.
- **Field numbers for a Sleep deck would understate it.**
