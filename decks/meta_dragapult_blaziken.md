**Not my list.** From Natalie Millar's *The Best Pokémon TCG Decks Right
Now (September 2026)* on TCGplayer (2026-08-25), reproduced card-for-card.

The published lists give collector numbers without set codes, so every
line was resolved by number against this repo's pool. Basic Energy is
written without a set code, as everywhere else here.

The Blaziken variant. `Blaziken ex` JTG 24's `Seething Spirit` attaches a Basic Energy from the discard once a turn, and per the article it is `stronger against Mega Excadrill since you hit for Fire weakness`. `Lillie's Clefairy ex`'s `Fairy Zone` is the mirror tech: it rewrites the Weakness of every opposing **Dragon** to Psychic, which is what `Dragapult ex` is.
